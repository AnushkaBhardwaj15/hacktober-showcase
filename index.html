FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We’ve got answers.

Is this event free to join? +
Yes! Opensource × Hacktoberfest 2026 organized by ACM Medicaps University is 100% free for all students.
Do I need prior coding or Git experience? +
No prior open-source experience is required! We will cover everything from setting up your GitHub account to creating your first pull request.
How will I join the session on 21st September? +
The session will take place live on Zoom Meet from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM IST. The meeting link and calendar invite will be sent directly to your registered email address.
Will I get a certificate or digital badge? +
Yes! All registered participants who attend the session and generate their registration pass will receive an official ACM Medicaps digital certificate of participation.
What is Hacktoberfest? +
Hacktoberfest is a month-long global celebration of open-source software held every October, encouraging developers of all skill levels to make meaningful pull requests.
