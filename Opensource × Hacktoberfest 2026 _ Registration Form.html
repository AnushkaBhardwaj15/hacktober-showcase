🚀 Start Your Open Source Journey

  ACM Medicaps University presents  

⭐ Opensource × Hacktoberfest 2026

Take your first step into the world of Open Source! Join this practical session to understand how the open-source ecosystem works, get comfortable with GitHub, and learn how to make your first meaningful contribution.

💡 What you’ll walk away with:
✅ A clear understanding of what Open Source is and how it works
✅ Practical knowledge of GitHub and open-source project structure
✅ How to find beginner-friendly projects worth contributing to
✅ A step-by-step understanding of making your first contribution
✅ Open Source etiquette, contribution guidelines & common mistakes
✅ Insights into how Hacktoberfest can help build real-world development experience

🗓 Date: 21st September 2026
⏰ Time: 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM
📍 Platform: Zoom Meet
🎙 Speaker: Mr. Harsh Sahu

📌 Who should attend?
Students who want to explore Open Source, contribute to real-world projects, get comfortable with GitHub, or take their first step beyond college projects.

✨ No prior Open Source experience required!

⚡ Learn the ecosystem. Make your first contribution.
